## Result

- **Declared reading that applies:** "white at 0.05 s does not reach, but a
  correlated or held process does".
  - Per-decision white noise never tacks: 0/300.
  - 11 cells reach, the best being pink noise, 0.5 s hold, σ = 0.3: 25%
    tacks, 3% failures.
- **Beyond the readings:** no cell makes upwind progress (mean max y ≤ 5.6 m;
  ≤ 2% of episodes reach y = 20 m), and none finishes (0/3,700).

  run the pilot in the foreground.
