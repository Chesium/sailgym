## Contract details

- All budgets count actual 20 Hz decisions, including the unchanged 10,000
  frozen-start smooth-random calibration decisions. Normalization is identical
  to M4U for a seed. B cells pack calibration into held-action transitions for
  SAC replay, splitting at action changes and episode boundaries.
- Training starts alternate frozen/random (exactly half, up to a final partial
  episode). Random headings are uniform and checked again against all held-out
  condition keys. Wind continues the original training stream after calibration.
- Scenario x/y change; the original route/gate, reward, time budget and physics
  do not. There is no simulator state restore.
- Block reward is the discounted sum of up to 10 original rewards. A block
  stops at any episode boundary or final collection-budget cutoff. Each block's
  actual discount is used for bootstrap, including partial timeouts.
- Raw actions/returns are stored at 20 Hz, with `m5_start_xy` metadata.
  `m5.replay_episode` reconstructs random initial positions before calling the
  existing exact replay checker. Existing generic replay works for evaluations.
- The neither cell's new seeds dispatch to the original trainer/config.
  B/S/BS reuse the numerical PPO/SAC functions with a new collection loop.
- PPO checkpoints follow the original rule: evaluate at the next completed
  rollout after each target. Final budget is exact. A final PPO rollout smaller
  than 256 uses its actual size as the minibatch; this is explicit in config.
- B/BS saved policies must be evaluated through `m5.HeldAction(policy, 10)`.
  The original `evaluate.py` CLI alone would omit the action hold.
