## Deviations from the plan text, recorded before results

- **Open water** is a symmetric ±80 m box, with y from −80 m to D + 60 m. U's box is asymmetric (x up to 120 m) and its y limit of 100 m is shorter than a 150 m leg. Under a rotated wind an asymmetric box has no meaning. The corridor features read 50 m for open water, so the box walls are unobserved, as in U.
- **The panel is stratified**: 5 bearing bins × 4 training widths × 2 legs. Wind speed, D and start are drawn from the training distribution. A plain random draw would hold about 3 dead-upwind legs in 40.
- **The curriculum** works on the width set. Stage k trains on widths {∞, 30, 15, 10}[: k+1], uniformly. The next width is added when the panel, rerun with every leg at the newest width, finishes ≥ NEED/40 (scaled to the panel size for specialists). With `--backoff N`, the newest width is dropped after N consecutive newest-width panels below NEED/2. M7 used 0/40, but in M8 the reach legs never fail.
- **P3** (DAgger distillation) is cut first, as the plan's cut order allows. **P2** runs at 2 × 256 only, specialists per bearing range (upwind 0–60°, reach 60–120°, downwind 120–180°) over the full width distribution. It is widened only if P1 shows a width effect.
- **Tack counting:** the frozen classifier is unchanged. Gybes (a flip with the stern within 80° of the wind) and x-reversals with 2 m hysteresis are counted alongside it.
- **Feature scaling:** M6's fixed scales, with dy (40 ± 40 m), the corridor edges (25 ± 25 m) and the now-rotated wind (0 ± 3.5 m/s) rescaled. Clip at 10.

## Tests (05:25)

`tests/test_m8_leg.py`, 5 pass:
- at β = 0, D = 40 m, open water, at rest and with U's bounds and budget, `LegVec` reproduces `TaskEnv(U)` rewards, states and outcome bit for bit;
- features are invariant when course and wind rotate together. Leg-frame features agree to 1e-6 over 250 decisions at four rotations. Rounding differences start at 1e-16 and grow about 1000× per 100 decisions under the sharp test policy (measured: 1e-12 by 200 decisions, 1e-9 by 300), so the comparison is bounded at 250;
- a one-leg route through the wrapper gives the same episode record and state hash as the single leg;
- stored episodes replay exactly, and a perturbed action changes the hash;
- RuleSailor sails the triangle through `RouteVec`. Rust finishes it, and the wrapper's leg index rises 0 → 1 → 2 on the way. `guidance.distance_to_target` is a look-ahead point, not the mark, so it cannot be used as this check.
