# M6 first campaign · Reverse-start pilots on U

**Both pilots stopped at the first band, 1–3 m below the gate.** On November 19, 2026, PPO and SAC each completed 400,000 raw decisions from scratch on seed 100, including the common 10,000-decision calibration. The campaign took **8 min 33 s** of its 150-minute wall-time ceiling, including controls, original-start validation and replay. The longer five-seed PPO batch depended on a successful pilot. Every 25 completed current-stage training episodes triggered a fresh deterministic probe. Promotion required at least 6/20 finishes on two successive probes, each exceeding both control rates. From the same 100 training-only control starts in the first band, neutral controls finished 0/100 and pink noise finished 4/100. With both learners below the promotion threshold, the campaign closed after the pilots. The five-seed comparison and held-out test/shift evaluation were conditional on a qualifying main run. The two 40 m results above are validation of the fixed final pilot checkpoints. **One PPO tack led to a gate crossing.** At 143,548 collected decisions, PPO started 2.9 m below the gate and 2.5 m east of its center. It sailed west and initially lost ground. The classifier marked the start of a settled tack 8.15 s into the episode, 5.1 m below the gate and 4.8 m west of center. The gate lay about 40° from the wind bearing. The boat came out of the turn with forward speed and finished at 35.33 s. The next three probes produced zero finishes on 60 fresh starts. This episode shows that PPO could execute a useful turn. Its frequency and the subsequent probe scores leave timing transfer unresolved. [PPO audit, episode ep0000087](../../runs/ese3990/M6/first/pilot/ppo_s100/audit.jsonl)

**The first bottleneck was a repeatable final approach.** PPO timed out on 67/100 near-gate probe episodes and crossed the gate line outside the marks on 16/100. SAC timed out on 65/80 and missed the marks on 7/80. Across the 40 original-start validation episodes, neither final policy recorded a settled tack. SAC's logged critic extrema spanned −35.23 to +14.00. The pilots used one seed, with no seed-matched M5 main comparison. [Pilot results and diagnostic counts](M6_first_results.json)

**Transfer coverage.** The setup replay measured median ground-track angles relative to wind of −52.8° and +52.8° on qualifying control segments. Under the frozen ±10 m lateral reset range, the generator produced 20 paired probe episodes per band for 1–3, 3–7 and 7–15 m. The two farther random bands lacked a full balanced panel under the geometric criteria. Neither pilot reached the first promotion, so the before/after transfer panels have zero learner observations. [Geometry and frozen panels](../../runs/ese3990/M6/first/freeze.json)

PPO took 49 s, including 18 s of promotion probing. SAC took 7 min 1 s, including 20 s of promotion probing. The audit exactly reproduced **868 stored episodes**, comprising 400 controls, 250 PPO records and 218 SAC records. These counts include calibration, partial training episodes, probes and final validation.
